"Life Of Chai is where good vibes & great chai meet ☕"

Specialties: Adrak Chai, Elaichi Chai, Bun Maska, Maggi & Coffee.

Location: Tiranga Square, Manewada Road, Nagpur.

Timings: 8:00 AM – 10:00 PM

Reputation: Loved by customers for taste, cleanliness & ambiance.
